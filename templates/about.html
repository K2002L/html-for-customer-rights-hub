ABOUT US

Who Are We?

Consumer Rights Hub is an inclusive online consumer rights protection platform designed to help users resolve e-commerce disputes more easily. We focus on clear navigation, barrier-free design, and practical support from complaint submission to progress tracking and community guidance.

01

Convenient Complaint Application

File your consumer rights claim and upload supporting evidence to resolve your dispute.

02

Real-time Progress Tracking

Check the status of submitted cases and understand the next step in the process.

03

Community and Support Hub

Browse real user cases, access legal guidance, and connect with human support options.

Meet Our Team

This platform is developed by a student team from UNSW Sydney. The project combines UX design, accessibility thinking, and consumer rights research.

The prototype was refined using usability feedback so the layout, labels, and interactions are easier to understand.
