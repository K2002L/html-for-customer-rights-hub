Fraud & Information Leakage

Immediate assistance for online fraud, identity theft, unauthorized charges, and data breaches.

⚠ Emergency Response Steps
  1. Secure Your Accounts Immediately Change passwords and enable multi-factor authentication on all financial accounts.
  2. Contact Your Bank or Card Issuer Report unauthorized charges immediately and request a freeze or replacement if needed.
  3. Document Everything Take screenshots of suspicious messages, transactions, and communication. Save all evidence.
  4. Report to Authorities File reports with local law enforcement and the FTC. Keep case numbers for reference.

Common Fraud Scenarios

Phishing Scams

Fake emails or texts pretending to be from legitimate companies requesting personal information.

Identity Theft

Unauthorized use of your personal information to open accounts or make purchases.

Payment Fraud

Unauthorized charges on credit cards, digital wallets, or bank accounts.

Data Breaches

Personal information exposed through company security failures or hacking incidents.

Fraud Prevention Tips

Protect Your Information

  • Never share passwords or PINs with anyone.
  • Use strong, unique passwords for each account.
  • Enable two-factor authentication everywhere possible.
  • Monitor bank statements and credit reports regularly.

Recognize Warning Signs

  • Urgent requests for personal or financial information.
  • Suspicious links or attachments in emails.
  • Offers that seem too good to be true.
  • Pressure to act immediately without verification.

Official Reporting Channels

Federal Trade Commission (FTC) Report fraud and identity theft → Internet Crime Complaint Center (IC3) File online fraud reporting → Local Law Enforcement Get guidance on filing a police report →
